Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Application and operator

SPY Research Reports is a personal-use research tool operated by Chance Skinner. It prepares descriptive SPY market research reports and emails them from the operator's authorized Gmail account to that same account. It is not offered for public account registration.

Google account access

The application requests only the Gmail send-only scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. This permission is used to send research report emails and attachments through the Gmail API.

The application does not request permission to read or search existing Gmail messages, access Google Contacts, or access Google Drive. It does not collect the operator's Google password. Google handles account sign-in and authorization.

Information used and stored

The application uses the configured sender and recipient email address, report subjects, email bodies, and report attachments to deliver research emails. OAuth access and refresh tokens enable authorized Gmail API access.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the operator's private computing environment, not in this public website repository. The workflow also stores research files and delivery-attempt records, including send status and Gmail message identifiers when returned by Google.

Purpose and sharing

Google account access is used only to deliver the operator's research reports and maintain delivery records. Email contents and attachments are transmitted to Google for delivery through Gmail. Credentials and operational files are processed in the operator's private computing environment, including the hosted server used to run the workflow.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. This public website does not publish OAuth tokens, private research reports, or delivery logs.

SPY Research Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

Security

Credential and token files on the research server are restricted using operating-system file permissions. Access to the server uses SSH authentication. These measures reduce unauthorized access risk but do not guarantee absolute security.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

OAuth tokens are retained to support authorized report delivery. Research files and delivery records remain stored until the operator deletes them; the current workflow does not provide an automatic deletion schedule.

The operator can stop future Google account access by removing the application's connection in Google Account security settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete local files or previously sent emails.

To remove application-held data, the operator can delete stored credentials, tokens, research files, and delivery records from the computing environments where they are kept. Previously delivered emails must be managed separately in Gmail. Privacy or deletion questions can be sent to the contact below.

Public website

This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages do not include application-added analytics, advertising, sign-in forms, or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical information associated with site visits under its own privacy statement .

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the application's data-handling practices change. The effective date will identify the latest revision.
