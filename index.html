SPY Research Reports

SPY Research Reports is a personal-use research tool operated by Chance Skinner. It collects market-data snapshots, prepares descriptive research reports about SPY options, and emails those reports to the operator's own Gmail account.

How the application works

Report collection and preparation take place in the operator's private computing environment. This public website provides application information and a privacy policy. It does not provide account registration or access to private reports.

Google account access

The application requests the Gmail send-only permission (gmail.send) to deliver research emails and attachments from the operator's authorized Gmail account to that same account. It does not request permission to read the inbox, search existing messages, or access Google Drive or Google Contacts.

This website does not ask visitors to sign in to Google or submit Google account credentials.

Research limitations

Reports describe saved market-data snapshots. They are not continuously current market data, executable prices, investment advice, or recommendations to trade.
